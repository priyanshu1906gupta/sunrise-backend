A renowned educational institution offering high-quality coaching for agriculture exams and school boards, rated 4.9 based on 144 reviews.

Location and infrastructure

We are at 22, Vishvsakha Colony, in front of Data Hanuman Mandir, near German Bhawan, Nutan Nagar, Khargone-451001. Students from across the city and nearby towns reach us easily.

Classrooms are equipped for interactive learning — smartboards, AC rooms, a well-stocked library and a professional, welcoming atmosphere so students can focus.

Classes offered

Courses are designed by faculty who bring classroom experience and exam insight:

  • Higher education: ICAR, UG agriculture programmes
  • School curriculum: CBSE, State Board — Class X, XI, XII
  • Exams: AIEEA, PAT, CPAT, BHU, CUET, B.Sc entrance
  • Subjects: Agriculture, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Maths, Science, Arts
