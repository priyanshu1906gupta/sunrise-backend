About Sunrise Coaching

A renowned institute in Nutan Nagar, Khargone for agriculture exams and school boards — rated 4.9 based on 144 reviews.

Established 7 July 2007

Welcome to Sunrise Coaching Khargone

Sunrise Coaching Classes in Nutan Nagar is a trusted destination for Tutorials for Agriculture Exam and a wide range of academic and competitive preparations. The campus sits in front of Data Hanuman Mandir, with modern classrooms, smartboards and a focused learning environment.

Students join us for Class 10–12 (CBSE and State Board) alongside PAT, CPAT, BHU, AIEEA and CUET-ICAR. Expert faculty, weekly tests, doubt sessions and updated study material are part of everyday coaching.

Learn More

Knowledge for life, not marks alone

Director Shri Amit Rane reminds students that education is not only a rank or a score. What we learn must serve society and the country. A successful person is not someone who only stores knowledge, but someone who uses it to help more people and take part in the nation’s progress.

Healthy competition here is meant to sharpen the mind — so every student works to become better than yesterday.
